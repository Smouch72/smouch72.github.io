Open geojson.io

Export writes a GeoJSON .txt file to your downloads — both points plus the track between them. Open geojson.io and drag the file onto the map. The site needs an internet connection; everything else here works offline.

Paste into the JSON editor pane on the right-hand side of geojson.io — it plots as you paste. Manual address: https://geojson.io
Range & bearing model: spherical earth, R = 6371 km, nm = km ÷ 1.852 — identical to the legacy analysis spreadsheet, so results are directly comparable.
WGS-84 check: the small figure under each distance is the Vincenty ellipsoid solution. Divergence is a few metres at short range and grows with distance; use it if your range instrumentation is WGS-84 referenced.
Export format: GeoJSON FeatureCollection — a point for each position carrying its name, colour and symbol, plus a LineString between them labelled with the range in nautical miles.
Word table: Export writes a .docx holding the Ship Readings table — same grid, borders and Times New Roman as the report template, with the Ship Reading and Differential cells left empty to fill in later. Open it and copy the table straight into your report.
Bearings are initial great-circle bearings, true (not magnetic). Relative bearing = true bearing − ship's head, measured clockwise from the bow.
