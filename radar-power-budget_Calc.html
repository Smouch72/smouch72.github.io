FSPL uses 20·log₁₀(RNM) + 20·log₁₀(fMHz) + 37.80, where 37.80 dB is the standard 32.44 dB kilometre constant plus 20·log₁₀(1.852) for nautical miles. Average power is Ppeak + 10·log₁₀(duty). Far-field boundary is 2D²/λ; inside it the path loss term does not hold. Polarisation, mismatch and ground-reflection terms are deliberately excluded.
